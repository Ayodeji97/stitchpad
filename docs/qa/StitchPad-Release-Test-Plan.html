1   Staff roles & multi-user workshop flagship

A workshop owner can invite teammates. Staff get a reduced, money-free, read-only work view — they see garments, statuses, dates, measurements and customer names, and can advance production, but never see prices, balances, payments, costs, profit, receipts, or customer contact. Test with owner = Fola, staff = Gabby.

2   Founding Tailors leaderboard new

A monthly member-get-member leaderboard for tailors. Each tailor gets a personal invite link; referrals that reach the "qualified" milestone earn tiered points; top 3 each month win a branded shirt. Test as an owner account (owner-gated surface).

IDTest stepExpected resultAndiOSResult
FT-01Owner: open Founding Tailors (dashboard card, or Settings → Invite & rewards which now leads with Founding Tailors).Screen loads with the tailor's own invite link and a Share (WhatsApp) action.
FT-02Look at the Your standing card.Shows a This month row and an All time row. A rank chip appears only when rank > 0. At 0/0 it shows the "Share your link to start earning" hint. Brief loading placeholder before it fills.
FT-03Read the How points work section (scroll).Five bullets explaining tiered points (+1 activation, +1 per active day, capped), monthly counting, and that installs/signups alone do not count.
FT-04Tap Share.WhatsApp share sheet opens with the invite link to getstitchpad.com/founding-tailors?code=<code>.
FT-05View the leaderboard (top rankings).Ranked list of top tailors; #1 rank reads as a tinted pill, distinct from the points figure.
FT-06Turn off network, then open Founding Tailors.The Your standing card is simply absent — no error banner — and link / share / leaderboard still work.

3   To collect new

A place to see who still owes money — Ready/Delivered orders with a balance remaining. Overdue = 7+ days. Includes an instant push when an order first becomes collectible. Test as an owner.

IDTest stepExpected resultAndiOSResult
TC-01Owner dashboard: find the You're owed / Money to collect card. Tap it.Card reflects Ready and Delivered unpaid orders and opens the To collect list.
TC-02In the list, use the sort menu and the filter chips.Sort: oldest-owed (default), biggest-balance, newest, customer A–Z; overdue always on top. Chips filter by overdue / status / customer.
TC-03Tap Chase on a row.WhatsApp opens with a balance-aware payment reminder signed with the workshop/business name. A customer with a blank/missing phone shows a snackbar instead of crashing.
TC-04Tap a row body.Opens that order's detail (to record payment).
TC-05Have an overdue (7+ days) unpaid order.Overdue collection is promoted into the dashboard focus hero; the Money-to-collect card shows a periodic nudge animation.
TC-06Move an order to Ready/Delivered while it still has a balance (with push enabled for your tester account).An instant push arrives once (not on every status change). Tapping it opens that order to record payment.
TC-07Tap the daily summary push (if received).Opens the To collect list.

Note: push notifications are tester-gated (staged rollout). If your account is not in the rollout, TC-06/TC-07 may not fire — mark N/A and note it.

4   In-app ratings new

Happy, engaged users are asked to rate at a delight moment (a recorded payment or an order marked Delivered). Happy → native store review; unhappy → feedback form. Use the Debug menu to force the prompt without waiting for the eligibility gate.

IDTest stepExpected resultAndiOSResult
RV-01Debug menu → Force review prompt → choose Love it 😍.The native in-app review UI appears (Play in-app review on Android, StoreKit on iOS).
RV-02Force the prompt again → choose Not really 🙁.Opens the Tally feedback form in the browser (tally.so/r/5BgVVb).
RV-03Settings → Help & Support → Rate StitchPad.Opens the app's store listing (write-review) on both platforms.
RV-04Debug → Reset review signals. On a fresh account, record a payment.Prompt does not show yet (gate: needs ~3 days installed, opened on 2+ days, 3+ orders). After thresholds are met, one payment shows it once; a second payment in the same session does not re-show.
RV-05Android release build only: smoke the Love-it path.Play in-app review flow behaves (OS may throttle the actual card — confirm no crash / no burned state).—

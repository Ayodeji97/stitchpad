FOR THE COORDINATOR ONLY

The printed booklet is enough to run and record the first supervised session.
Do not ask the tailor or participant to edit JSON or run evaluation software.

After the session, transcribe deidentified records into a PRIVATE copy of
protocol-template.json. Keep raw paper forms; do not overwrite disagreements.

1. Enter the protocol ID, approved_by, limits and frozen=true only after the lead
   tailor has approved the protocol. Do not use guessed values. Use development
   for rehearsal participants. Never move people into held-out evaluation after
   seeing their results or place the same person in multiple partitions.
2. Add participants with participant_id, split, consent_id and references for
   chest, waist, hips. Each reading has measurer_id, repeat, value_mm and blinded.
   The paper sheets use cm: multiply by 10 for value_mm. Never declare blinded=true
   unless app estimates and the other measurer's results were actually hidden.
3. Before each capture, registered_attempts needs attempt_id, participant_id and
   engine_version from pipeline-plans.json for the planned A/E/M pair. Paper page 6
   is the source initiation log. Include every started attempt. Do not rewrite an
   earlier failed automatic attempt when doing an edited/manual fallback.
4. outcomes bind attempt_id to completed/failed/abandoned. Completed attempts attach
   the actual image-free export as result; failed/abandoned attempts attach no result.
   Include the failure reason in the private source log even though the evaluator
   output summarizes counts. Missing outcomes are treated as failed.
5. If references disagree beyond their pre-agreed limit, preserve the raw sheets.
   A third independent blinded measurer can repeat the disputed field twice on a
   copy of page 4 labelled C. Record their ID and reason. Do not adjust the original
   values to force agreement. If unresolved, stop that reference's analysis; do not
   silently drop it from the study.
6. An optional participant adjudications object maps the disputed field to:
   measurer_id, blinded=true, reason and readings_mm (at least two independent values).
   The third measurer must differ from the original measurers. The agreed spread
   limit also applies to the new readings. The source records remain unchanged.
7. Run the bundled code only on private files outside the kit or a Git checkout:
   python3 coordinator/build_ledger.py /private/study.json /private/new-ledger.json
   python3 coordinator/evaluate.py /private/new-ledger.json
   Windows: use the Python 3 launcher and appropriate private filesystem paths.

The evaluator intentionally reports held-out evaluation attempts only. A development
rehearsal with no evaluation partition does not generate an accuracy certification.
The paper comparison can still reveal issues to fix. Do not pretend a small pilot
represents all body shapes, phones, countries or garment outcomes.

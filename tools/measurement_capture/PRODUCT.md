# Local measurement research prototype

## Product and audience
StitchPad serves tailors and their customers. This isolated research surface tests
customer self-capture and designer-assisted capture for remote body measurements.
Daniel approved both journeys, multiple images, accuracy before convenience and an
owned low-cost engine. It must not write estimates into customer measurements.

## Platform and stack
Local browser prototype, plain HTML/CSS/ES modules; no build service, paid API or
photo upload. Uses existing Adire Atelier colours and native system fonts offline.
This is a research tool, not the approved production customer discovery interface.

## Scope and evidence
Front/side images plus known height and explicit anatomical outline annotation.
The ellipse baseline is a new experimental geometric method, not trained ML or
validated tailoring inference. Manual annotation is deliberate until a licensed
segmentation/fitting model passes evaluation. No accuracy tolerance is approved.

## Constraints
EN/FR, keyboard access, small screens, no analytics/network fonts/photo persistence.
Self-reported capture checks cannot verify pose, clothing or camera calibration.
A consenting tape-reference cohort and designer review are required before release.

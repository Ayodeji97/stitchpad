Recommendation: Option A (top card)

  • · Above the fold, zero scroll. A staff member sees it the instant the screen opens, right under the subtitle, instead of hunting past Continue + Skip.
  • · Reads as a distinct path, not a footnote. The indigo-tinted card + badge icon + "Enter code ›" makes it obviously tappable and clearly separate from the owner setup form below it.
  • · Doesn't disrupt owners. It's one compact card; owners glance past it and fill the form as before. (We could even auto-hide it once they start typing a business name.)
  • · Option B (a real outlined button at the bottom) is better than today's gray text, but it is still below the fold, so discoverability is only half-fixed.
  • · Both navigate to the same RedeemInvite screen already wired in Slice 7 — this is purely a placement/prominence change on WorkshopSetup.

My recommendation

Ship Option A — with a borrowed touch from B

Option A is the right default: it fixes exactly what you flagged (flat hierarchy, no clickability) with the least disruption and stays true to the Adire Atelier system. The three cards keep their proven scannability, urgency colour gives them a real pecking order, and the segmented pipeline retires four noisy tiles for one calm bar.

Borrow one thing from B: when a card's count is 0, soften it to a plain neutral (no red), and when everything is clear, let the "All caught up" state carry a small directive line — so the screen still guides rather than just reporting zeros.

Clickability — the important nuance. "Overdue: 2" is two orders, so a tap must open the Orders list filtered to that status, not a single order. Same behaviour whether the count is 1 or 20 — consistent and scalable.

Tap behaviour

  • 2 →Overdue → Orders, filtered Overdue, newest-late first
  • 1 →Due today → Orders, filtered Due today
  • 3 →In progress → Orders, filtered In progress

Build order

1 · re-style the 3 cards (urgency tokens + "View →").
2 · swap 4 pipeline tiles → segmented bar.
3 · wire each card to the filtered Orders route.
4 · 0-count + all-caught-up states.

Mockups use a system-serif stack to stand in for the production faces — Fraunces (display), Manrope (body), JetBrains Mono (counts). Colours are the real Adire Atelier tokens; urgency (red / amber / indigo) is a semantic scale kept separate from the Saffron heritage accent. Renders in light & dark.

One functional caveat: tapping a card → filtered list → an order detail will still hit "order not found" for staff until Slice 8d strips money/contact from the base docs. The UI redesign and the 8d migration are independent — this page is about the former.

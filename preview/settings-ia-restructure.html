Settings — information architecture options

Same design system, four structures. The goal: a landing screen you can take in at a glance that still has a home for what's coming (Team / Staff, richer notifications). Frequently-flipped preferences stay one tap; low-frequency management drills down.

How I'd ship it

  • Behind your rollout flag — new IA gated by rollout.ts (staging + allowlist first, then a % of users, kill switch on standby). Old screen stays compiled behind the flag so a revert is instant.
  • No A/B experiment — Settings has no clean success metric and too little traffic to reach significance. Gradual rollout gives ~90% of the safety at ~5% of the cost.
  • Reuses what exists — each drill-down is just another Root/Screen pair in feature/settings/presentation/, new @Serializable routes in the settings nav graph. Rows and section cards are already components (SettingsRow, SettingsSectionCard).

The model (how it hangs together)

  • · Team roster — a new list under the workshop (users/{workshopUid}/team/{memberId}): the owner + staff account(s) auto-appear, plus name-only members (Paul, Tunde…) the owner/admin adds. No login needed to be assignable.
  • · Assignment = one field on the order (assignedMemberId + denormalised name/colour for display). Whole-order for now; per-garment/stage can layer on later.
  • · Who can assign — owner AND the staff admin (write the assignee, like they already write status). "Claim it" just assigns to yourself.
  • · Not sensitive — assignee isn't money or contact, so it lives on the base order doc and both roles read/write it; no change to the money/contact wall.
  • · Live status — assignee + production stage together answer "who's on it, and where." Cards show the assignee avatar; the order detail shows an Assign row.
  • · Dashboard — "Assigned to me" for app users (owner/staff); a name-only member like Paul has no dashboard, they just appear as the assignee.
  • · Slice — its own slice after the shell (roster CRUD + assign field + rules + the assign UI + dashboard "My work"); 6b's dashboard is being designed to make room for the "Assigned to me" section.

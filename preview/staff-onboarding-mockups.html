Why this direction

  • · Two screens only. RedeemInviteRoute (join) and StaffPendingRoute (waiting). Both reuse the ForgotPassword MVI shape (Root + Screen + State/Action/Event).
  • · Deep link prefills the code. A JOIN_WORKSHOP target on the existing deep-link holder drops the staffer straight onto "Join · from link" with the code filled — one tap to join.
  • · Passive pending screen. No "check status" button needed — the session provider (Slice 5a) watches the membership doc and flips the whole app to the staff shell the instant the owner approves. The screen just reflects that wait.
  • · Owner-of-self is the safe fallback. A pending staffer stays on their own (empty) tree; no owner data is addressable until the approval claim lands — enforced by rules, not just the UI.
  • · Redeem persists the workshopUid to StaffMembershipPrefs so the pending screen and auto-approval work even across an app restart (this wires the 5a plumbing).
  • · Leave / Sign out both clear the stored workshopUid so a shared device never inherits a stale membership.
  • · Indigo, not saffron. Adire Atelier primary throughout; saffron only as the heritage tick on the crest and the "awaiting approval" pulse dot.

What the staff member sees

The staff dashboard is a work view, not a money view. No revenue, no "ready to collect", no weekly earnings goal, no collect nudges — those never enter the staff state. Instead: what needs attention today (by count), a prioritised queue of orders to advance through production (customer name only, no contact), and the pipeline by stage. Three tabs, no Reports.

Why this direction

  • · Money-free at the STATE level. Staff DashboardState never carries outstandingAmount, nextBestActions (collect nudges), or weeklyGoal — the VM simply doesn't populate them when isActiveStaff. Not hidden in the composable; absent.
  • · Reuses the money-free signals that already exist. outstandingOrderCount / overdueCount / pipeline counts are already count-based in the owner state — the staff view leans on those.
  • · Work, not revenue. The hero is "what needs attention" (overdue / due today by count) + a queue of orders to advance. No "ready to collect" hero, no earnings goal.
  • · Customer name only. Rows show the garment + customer name + due date + status. No phone, no contact actions — that's Slice 6c's suppression.
  • · Three tabs. Dashboard / Customers / Orders — no Reports (Slice 6a).
